# T047 System Status — uploaders on the acquisition PCs, and storage
**Abstract:**
This document contains the definitions for tests that target the feature 'uploader health and storage on the System Status page' (DR-033): the heartbeats the Export Watcher and the Optomed Bridge send from the acquisition PCs, how the page classifies them, the arrivals per device, and the hourly storage measurement.

These are the acceptance tests an operator runs after installing or updating the uploaders on the Clarus, Spectralis and Optomed PCs.

**prerequisite (all tests):**
Logged in as an Administrator. The Export Watcher and the Optomed Bridge in their version of 24 September 2026 or later, installed on their PCs as described in the deploy README. Open **System → Systemstatus**.

---

## T047-01: each uploader appears under its PC's name
**description:**
Every upload program reports every two minutes, switched on or not. After installation it must appear without anybody registering it.

**workflow:**
1. on each acquisition PC, run `ExportWatcher.ps1 -Heartbeat` (Clarus and Spectralis PCs) and `OptomedBridge.ps1 -Heartbeat` (the Optomed PC)
1. open **Systemstatus** and find the panel **Uploader an den Aufnahme-PCs**

**expected results:**
each command prints `recorded` (**check A**)
one row per program, named after its PC (or after *Name on the status page* where that is set), with the program and its version beneath (**check B**)
every running, switched-on program reads **In Ordnung** within two minutes (**check C**)

## T047-02: a closed program and a shut-down PC read differently
**description:**
A PC switched off in the evening is not an outage; a program somebody closed is.

**workflow:**
1. on the Spectralis PC, right-click the Export Watcher's tray icon and choose **Exit**
1. on the Clarus PC, log off Windows
1. refresh **Systemstatus**

**expected results:**
the Spectralis PC's watcher reads **Beendet**, in red (**check A**)
the Clarus PC's programs read **Abgemeldet oder heruntergefahren**, in grey (**check B**)
after logging back on at the Clarus PC, its programs read **In Ordnung** again within two minutes, without anybody touching the page (**check C**)

## T047-03: a program that goes silent is shown as silent
**description:**
A crashed program or a PC off the network says nothing at all. The page must notice the silence rather than keep showing the last good report.

**workflow:**
1. on the Spectralis PC, end the Export Watcher's `powershell.exe` process in the Task Manager (this sends no last report)
1. wait six minutes, then refresh **Systemstatus**

**expected results:**
the program reads **Keine Meldung**, with the time of its last report (**check A**)

## T047-04: problems are named
**description:**
"Needs attention" must say what needs it.

**workflow:**
1. on the Spectralis PC, rename the export folder the watcher watches
1. on the Optomed PC, close the Optomed Client (not the bridge)
1. wait two minutes, refresh **Systemstatus**
1. undo both

**expected results:**
the watcher reads **Braucht Aufmerksamkeit** with *der Exportordner fehlt* beneath it (**check A**)
the bridge reads **Braucht Aufmerksamkeit** with *der Optomed Client läuft nicht* beneath it (**check B**)
two minutes after undoing, both read **In Ordnung** again (**check C**)

## T047-05: files that wait or fail are visible
**description:**
A file that cannot be uploaded must not wait unnoticed on the PC.

**workflow:**
1. on the Clarus PC, switch the Export Watcher off (**Enabled** unticked) and export one capture
1. after two minutes, refresh **Systemstatus**
1. switch the watcher on again and wait for the next sweep

**expected results:**
while switched off: **Ausgeschaltet**, and *1 wartend* (or more) in the files column (**check A**)
after switching on: the files column returns to *0 wartend*, the upload count of the day grows, and the arrivals table shows the Clarus row with a last file *vor … Sekunden/Minuten* (**check B**)

## T047-06: arrivals per device reflect every way in
**description:**
The arrivals table counts what reached the platform, whichever way it came.

**workflow:**
1. upload one JPEG through the upload page for a test subject
1. refresh **Systemstatus** and read **Eingänge je Gerät**

**expected results:**
a row for the device with the way in *Upload (Seite, Export Watcher, Bridge)*, and its 24-hour count one higher than before (**check A**)
rows for the other ways in that were used in the last 90 days (DICOM receiver, Remidio cloud, OCT upload) with their own counts (**check B**)

## T047-07: removing a replaced PC
**description:**
A PC that was replaced leaves a silent row behind; it can be removed.

**workflow:**
1. on a row that reads **Keine Meldung**, click **Entfernen** and confirm
1. repeat on the row of a program that is running

**expected results:**
the silent row disappears and stays away (**check A**)
the running program's row disappears and comes back within two minutes (**check B**)

## T047-08: storage is measured and shows its trend
**description:**
The page answers "how full is the disk, and how long until it is full" without an SSH session.

**workflow:**
1. read the panel **Speicherplatz**
1. click **Jetzt messen** and wait for *wird gemessen …* to disappear
1. after the platform has run for at least a week, read the panel again

**expected results:**
each disk shows its free and total space and the percentage used; the stores under it are listed with their path, size and file count, and the database with its size and largest tables (**check A**)
*gemessen* shows the time of the new measurement (**check B**)
after a week, the column *Änderung in 7 Tagen* is filled, and a disk whose free space shrank shows *voll in etwa … Tagen*; the figure agrees with `df -h` on the VM to within the growth of the last hour (**check C**)
