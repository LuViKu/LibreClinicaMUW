# T046 fundus image ingest — inbox and upload page
**Abstract:**
This document contains the definitions for tests that target the feature 'fundus image ingest' (DR-025, DR-029): the reconciliation inbox for files that arrive from a camera or the upload page, the unauthenticated upload page (Remidio JPEG, Clarus/PlexElite DICOM, Spectralis OCT), the staff uploader behind the inbox, and the DICOM path used by the Optomed Lumo.

These are the acceptance tests an operator runs before the HealthAEye study goes live. The technical dry run (`deploy/dicom-dry-run.sh`) proves the wiring; these prove the workflow.

**prerequisite (all tests):**
A study subject enrolled in the study the camera serves, with a visit scheduled for today. You must know that subject's label. Open the subject in the application: the strip above the visits must read **Heute auf der Kamera-Worklist** — if it reads *Nicht auf der Kamera-Worklist*, the visit is not on today's schedule, and the strip's button puts it there (T046-13 tests the strip itself). For the inbox tests you must be logged in with a role of data manager, investigator, clinical research coordinator, or administrator.

---

## T046-01: an image from the camera binds itself to the scheduled visit
**description:**
The Optomed Lumo pulls a worklist, the operator picks the patient on the device, and the image that comes back carries the identity the worklist gave it. Nobody should have to reconcile it by hand.

**workflow:**
1. on the camera, open the worklist and refresh it
1. find today's visit for the test subject in the list
1. select it and take one image of the right eye
1. in the application, go to **Bilder-Eingang** (image inbox)

**expected results:**
the image does **not** appear in the inbox, because it was bound on arrival (**check A**)
the image appears on the visit, and the visit's CRF shows the camera's modality as performed (**check B**)

## T046-02: an image taken without picking a patient lands in the inbox
**description:**
An operator who photographs before selecting a worklist entry produces an image with no identity. It must not be discarded and must not attach itself to the wrong patient.

**workflow:**
1. on the camera, take an image **without** selecting a worklist entry
1. in the application, go to **Bilder-Eingang**

**expected results:**
the image appears in the inbox with the status "unbound" (**check A**)
the inbox shows the image's preview, the time it arrived and the device it came from (**check B**)

## T046-03: binding an inbox image to a visit
**description:**
The reconciliation step: an operator identifies the patient an unbound image belongs to.

**workflow:**
1. in **Bilder-Eingang**, open the unbound image from T046-02
1. search for the test subject by label
1. choose today's visit and the eye the image shows
1. confirm

**expected results:**
the image leaves the inbox (**check A**)
the image appears on the chosen visit, for the chosen eye (**check B**)
the audit trail records who bound it and when (**check C**)

## T046-04: dismissing an image that is not study data
**description:**
Test exposures and images of the wrong subject must be removable without deleting the record of that decision.

**workflow:**
1. in **Bilder-Eingang**, open an unbound image
1. choose **Verwerfen** (dismiss) and give a reason
1. confirm

**expected results:**
the image leaves the inbox (**check A**)
the audit trail records the dismissal, its reason and who made it (**check B**)

> The file itself is deleted by the nightly sweep once the retention window has passed, not immediately. See the deploy runbook, section 7.

## T046-05: uploading a Remidio image from a phone
**description:**
The Remidio InstaFOP exports a JPEG rather than speaking DICOM, so its images arrive through the upload page (`/LibreClinica/app/upload`), which needs no login — the operator is standing at the camera with a phone. The older addresses `/app/image-upload` and `/app/oct-upload` lead to the same page.

**prerequisite:**
The upload page's address, reachable from the phone. No account is needed.

**workflow:**
1. open the upload page on the phone
1. under **Visite für diese Dateien**, pick today's visit from the list, or use **Patient suchen** and pick the visit, or type the label and accept the visit offered
1. select the image; a row appears naming it **JPEG** or **PNG**
1. choose the eye on the row
1. **Bestätigen**

**expected results:**
the row shows the upload progress and then **Zugeordnet** with the visit (**check A**)
the image appears on that visit in the application, without passing through the inbox (**check B**)
a **Rückgängig** link on the row takes the upload back within a minute; afterwards it does not (**check C**)

## T046-06: uploading without identifying the visit
**description:**
The fallback path when the operator cannot find the visit: the image is still accepted, and reconciliation happens later.

**workflow:**
1. open the upload page
1. type the subject label as free text instead of picking a visit, and select an image
1. on the row choose **Ohne Visite hochladen**

**expected results:**
the row shows **Im Eingang** (**check A**)
the image appears in the **Eingang** as unbound, with the typed label shown as a hint (**check B**)

## T046-07: an unknown label reveals nothing
**description:**
The upload page is unauthenticated. It must not become a way to find out which subject labels exist.

**workflow:**
1. open the upload page
1. type a label that does not exist, e.g. `ZZZ-999`
1. type a single character, e.g. `M`

**expected results:**
the unknown label returns no match and no hint about which labels do exist (**check A**)
the single character is refused as too short rather than listing every subject beginning with it (**check B**)
no patient name, date of birth or sex is shown anywhere on the page (**check C**)

## T046-08: the upload page refuses a flood
**description:**
An unauthenticated page needs a limit, or a script can use it to fill the disk.

**workflow:**
1. from one device, search or resolve labels repeatedly — more than 30 times within an hour
1. then upload one more file to a visit already picked

**expected results:**
further lookups are refused with a message asking the operator to wait, not with a server error (**check A**)
the upload itself still goes through — uploads have their own, larger budget (**check B**)
the refusals stop once the hour has passed (**check C**)

## T046-09: the worklist offers only this study
**description:**
A handheld camera on a clinic bench is shared. A camera enrolled for one study must not display another study's schedule, because the worklist carries subject labels, sex and dates of birth.

**prerequisite:**
A second study exists with a visit scheduled today.

**workflow:**
1. on the camera, refresh the worklist

**expected results:**
only visits from the study the camera serves are listed (**check A**)
no subject from the other study appears (**check B**)

## T046-10: the internal endpoints are not reachable from outside
**description:**
The worklist and ingest endpoints are for the receiver on the server, not for the network.

**workflow:**
1. from a workstation, open `https://<site>/LibreClinica/pages/api/v1/internal/dicom-worklist`
1. from a workstation, try to connect to port 11112 on the server

**expected results:**
the address returns "not found" (**check A**)
the port refuses the connection or times out (**check B**)

## T046-11: uploading a Clarus (or PlexElite) DICOM export
**description:**
The Zeiss devices export `.dcm` files rather than speaking DICOM to the server. The page reads the eye and the date out of the file, and the file is stripped of the hospital's patient identity before it is kept — the visit's subject label takes its place.

**prerequisite:**
A DICOM export of a test eye from the device, on the phone or workstation. The `dicom` compose profile running (see the deploy runbook, §7): without it the page refuses DICOM files rather than keeping them unchanged.

**workflow:**
1. open the upload page and pick today's visit for the test subject
1. select the `.dcm` file(s) of the visit at once

**expected results:**
each row names the file **DICOM**, shows the device model, the eye and the acquisition date read from the file, and offers no eye selector (**check A**)
after **Bestätigen** the row reads **Zugeordnet · Header pseudonymisiert** (**check B**)
in the application the files appear on the visit for the right eyes; opening one from the export bundle shows the subject label as patient name and ID, no date of birth, no operator, no institution (**check C**)
with the `dicom` profile stopped, the same upload is refused with a message that DICOM uploads are not available — nothing appears in the Eingang (**check D**)

## T046-12: uploading from the inbox as a logged-in user
**description:**
A data manager filing a folder of exports at their desk uses the same uploader behind their login, and the upload is on their record.

**prerequisite:**
Logged in as data manager, investigator or administrator.

**workflow:**
1. open the **Eingang** and choose **Hochladen**
1. pick the visit from the day's list (this list is always available behind a login) or search the patient
1. select several files of different kinds and **Bestätigen** them together

**expected results:**
every row is filed against the visit (**check A**)
the audit trail records the logged-in user as the person who filed each file (**check B**)

## T046-13: the subject page says whether the camera will list the patient
**description:**
The camera's worklist is the visit schedule for today. Nobody should have to walk to the device to find out whether a patient is on it, or why not.

**prerequisite:**
A subject in the study the camera serves with **no** open visit today (a new enrolment will do), and a subject of a study the camera does not serve.

**workflow:**
1. open the first subject in the application
1. read the strip above the visits
1. click **Baseline für heute planen** (or **Auf heute verschieben** if a visit exists on another day)
1. on the camera, refresh the worklist
1. open the second subject

**expected results:**
before the click the strip reads **Nicht auf der Kamera-Worklist** and says why (**check A**)
after the click it reads **Heute auf der Kamera-Worklist**, naming the visit and an accession `LC…`, and the visit appears in the table below dated today (**check B**)
the camera lists the subject's label, and the accession on the device matches the strip (**check C**)
the second subject shows no strip at all (**check D**)

## T046-14: a Clarus export folder uploads itself
**description:**
The Export Watcher on the Clarus PC picks up every `.dcm` the photographer exports and files it, so nobody re-uploads by hand.

**prerequisite:**
The Export Watcher installed and enabled on the Clarus PC (deploy README, "Clarus and Spectralis exports"), the Clarus export pointed at its folder, and a subject whose label is the PatientID entered on the Clarus, with a visit today.

**workflow:**
1. on the Clarus, photograph both eyes of the test subject and export to the watched folder
1. wait for two sweeps (under a minute)
1. in the application, open the subject's visit

**expected results:**
the files leave the folder for `_uploaded\` within a minute of the export finishing (**check A**)
the images appear on today's visit, for the right eyes, without passing through the Eingang (**check B**)
the visit's CRF shows the Clarus modality as performed (**check C**)
the watcher's log names counts and the label only — no name, no filename (**check D**)

## T046-15: a Spectralis export lands per volume, or waits in the inbox
**description:**
The watcher on the Spectralis PC reads each `.e2e` itself: one upload per OCT volume, bound when the patient id is a label with one visit that day, parked in the inbox otherwise.

**prerequisite:**
The Export Watcher installed and enabled on the Spectralis PC. Two exports: one of a subject whose HEYEX patient id is their label with a visit today, and one whose patient id matches no subject.

**workflow:**
1. export both from HEYEX into the watched folder
1. wait for two sweeps
1. open the subject's visit, then the **Eingang**

**expected results:**
the first export appears on the visit as one scan per eye, each with the acquisition date read from the file, and inference starts for each (**check A**)
the second export is in the Eingang, unbound, one row per volume, with the typed id and date as hints and no inference started (**check B**)
both files are in `_uploaded\`; a file the watcher could not read after five sweeps would be in `_failed\`, named in the log by kind and attempt count only (**check C**)
